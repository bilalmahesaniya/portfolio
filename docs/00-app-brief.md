# App Brief

**App name:** Bilal Mahesaniya — Portfolio
*(working title; can be swapped for a personal brand name/tagline)*

**One-line idea:**
A dark, modern, single-scroll portfolio site that shows recruiters and hiring managers exactly what Bilal Mahesaniya can design — fast.

**Problem it solves:**
Recruiters and hiring managers spend seconds, not minutes, deciding whether a candidate is worth a closer look. Bilal needs one credible, well-organized place that proves his UI/UX and graphic design ability, backs it with real training/certification, and makes the next step (contact, resume, portfolio links) obvious.

**Who it is for:**
- Primary: Recruiters, hiring managers, and design leads evaluating Bilal for **junior UI/UX designer or graphic designer roles / internships**.
- Secondary: Freelance clients, fellow students, mentors, and instructors from Xipra Tech.

**The main action a user should complete:**
Scroll through the story (who Bilal is → what he's trained in → proof via 12 projects) and take one clear next step: **contact Bilal or download his resume.**

**Must-have features:**
- Hero/intro section (name, role, one-line pitch, primary CTA)
- About section with education timeline (12th Science → UI/UX certification → Graphic Design course → ongoing Diploma in Computer Engineering)
- Skills & tools section
- Project showcase — all 12 projects, filterable by **UI/UX Design** vs **Graphic Design**
- Individual project detail view (modal or expandable card)
- Certifications section
- Contact section (form + direct email + social/portfolio links)
- Resume download
- Fully responsive (mobile, tablet, desktop)

**Nice-to-have features:**
- Scroll-triggered motion/animation
- Case-study-style long-form pages for 2–3 flagship projects
- Light/dark toggle *(default direction is dark-first — see assumption below)*
- Testimonials/recommendations block
- Simple analytics (visits, resume downloads)

**Platform:**
Web — responsive across mobile / tablet / desktop. *(Single deployed site, no native app.)*

**Business model, if relevant:**
None — this is a personal career asset, not a commercial product. Primary goal is job/internship acquisition; a secondary benefit is attracting freelance inquiries.
